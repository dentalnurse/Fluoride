How it works

One account for all your CPD

👤

1. Create a free account

Register once with your name (and your GDC number if you are a dental professional). Every course you take is saved to the same account.

💳

2. Choose a course

Pay securely by card for the course you want. Your course is unlocked once your payment has been checked, within 1 working day.

🏅

3. Learn and get certified

Work through the lessons, pass the quiz, complete a short reflection and feedback form, then download your certificate.

CPD for everyone

Verifiable CPD courses are for GDC-registered dental professionals. Dental nurses need at least 50 hours of verifiable CPD in each 5-year cycle, and these certificates show the hours, GDC development outcomes, aims and objectives you need for your CPD record.

Courses marked CPD · GDC verifiable are open to everyone. Add your GDC number to your account and your certificate becomes a verifiable CPD certificate. Courses marked CPD are open to everyone and give a CPD certificate, but are not GDC verifiable.
