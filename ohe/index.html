Assessment

How you are assessed

No exams. Assessment is through MCQs, written tasks, your practical logbook, and a final reflective account. All submitted and reviewed online.

📝 MCQ - 15 questions per module

A 15-question multiple choice quiz at the end of each module. You must pass before moving to the next module. You can retake if needed.

✍️ Written tasks (x3)

Three written tasks on consent and record-keeping, your GDC responsibilities and scope of practice, and the evidence base for the preventive advice you give. Answered directly in your dashboard.

🦷 Practical logbook (10 entries)

Log each supervised oral health education session, including patient group, topics covered, consent, assessment checks, and advice given - countersigned by a GDC-registered clinical supervisor who was present.

📋 Case studies (x2)

Two extended case studies covering patient assessment, the education session delivered, advice and signposting given, and reflection. Written directly in your practical portfolio.

🪞 End-of-course reflective account (300–500 words)

A final reflective account drawing on your learning and practical experience across the course. Written directly in the platform or uploaded as a document.

GDC Scope of Practice · Verifiable CPD

GDC compliant and GDC verifiable CPD

Delivering oral health education and advice is recognised within the GDC Scope of Practice for dental nurses, provided they are appropriately trained, competent and indemnified, and work within a structured programme or under appropriate direction from the dentist, hygienist or therapist responsible for the patient's care. This qualification supports all three requirements. It remains the responsibility of each individual dental nurse to ensure they are trained, competent and appropriately indemnified before delivering oral health education independently.

This course provides 15 hours of verifiable CPD, mapped to GDC Development Outcomes A (Communication), C (Maintenance and development of knowledge and skill) and D (Patient safety and quality of care).
